There is now a new "Official" [Oracle wiki](http://wiki.oracle.com/) out there. This is pretty neat as it lets Oracle employees and everyone else to collaborate together. This will be cool from my point of view since we deal with [SQL Developer](http://www.oracle.com/technology/products/database/sql_developer/index.html) and the [Oracle Migration Workbench](http://www.oracle.com/technology/tech/migration//workbench/index_sqldev_omwb.html).  
  
The point of all this is that normally the most interaction we have is either through the forum ([SQL Developer](http://forums.oracle.com/forums/forum.jspa?forumID=260) and [Migration Workbench](http://forums.oracle.com/forums/forum.jspa?forumID=1)) or through [Support](http://www.oracle.com/support/index.html).  
  
Now we have the ability to add information about product and drop in loads of ideas, solutions and best practices from everyone.
